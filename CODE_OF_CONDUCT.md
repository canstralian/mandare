# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Mandare is a technical project. Contributors, reviewers, and maintainers are expected to make the project useful, safe, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

We expect:

- respectful, professional communication;
- criticism of ideas, code, and evidence rather than people;
- good-faith disagreement and clear reasoning;
- willingness to correct factual errors;
- careful handling of security-sensitive information;
- constructive review that explains the problem and, where practical, the path forward.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publication of private information without consent, and knowingly introducing malicious or deceptive changes.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, restrict participation, or take other reasonable action when behaviour violates this standard.

For conduct concerns, use the project's private reporting contact:

[Click here to contact the project maintainers](mailto:distortedprojection@gmail.com?subject=Mandare%20conduct%20concern)

Security vulnerabilities should be reported according to [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), not through public issues or discussions.

## Scope

This standard applies to project spaces and project-related interactions, including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and contributor communication.

The goal is not perfect agreement. The goal is a contributor environment where disagreement improves the system rather than degrading the people building it.
