# Knowledge Engine

## Mission

Transform resource observations into governed knowledge.

Knowledge is derived.

Knowledge is never authoritative.

---

## Pipeline

Resource

↓

Snapshot

↓

Projection

↓

Knowledge

↓

Documentation

---

## Validate

- snapshot integrity
- projection correctness
- synchronization manifests
- generated knowledge
- provenance
- evidence links

---

## Reject

- undocumented projections
- authoritative generated content
- hidden synchronization
- missing provenance

---

## Success Criteria

Generated knowledge is reproducible, governed, and fully traceable to authoritative sources.
